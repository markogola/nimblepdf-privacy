NimblePDF Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how NimblePDF for Android (package com.nimblepdf.app), published by Odhuno developers ("we"), handles your information. It covers the version on Google Play and the version on the Amazon Appstore. Where they differ, this page says so.

Your documents stay on your device

NimblePDF works on your device. PDFs, images, scans, signatures and extracted text are processed locally and stored in the app's private storage or in locations you choose. We do not upload your documents, and we cannot see them. We have no servers that receive your files.

If a future version offers a conversion that needs an online service, the app will ask for your consent for that document before anything is sent.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics or crash reporting of its own.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps file names, sizes, page counts, bookmarks, favourites, processing history and your settings in a local database, so the library can work. This data does not leave your device, and it is excluded from device backups. Uninstalling the app removes it, along with documents stored in the app.

Camera

The app asks for camera access only when you use the scanner. Photos are processed on your device. You can decline and import pictures instead. Devices without a camera are never asked.

Advertising

Amazon Appstore version: shows no ads. It never starts an advertising or analytics service and does not use advertising identifiers.

Google Play version: shows ads through Google AdMob: banners on the Home, Library and Settings tabs, an occasional full-screen ad after a finished tool, and rewarded ads that play only when you choose to watch one to unlock a job. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • app interactions with ads, crash logs and diagnostic information

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Data is encrypted in transit.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices in the app. On any Android device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's settings.

Text recognition

Text recognition uses Google ML Kit's on-device model. Images are not sent to Google for recognition. ML Kit may send anonymous diagnostic data about its own performance to Google.

Permissions

  • Camera: the document scanner, only when you open it.
  • Internet: used for ads in the Google Play version. Your documents never use the network.

Files are opened and saved through the Android file picker, so the app only gets access to the files you choose. The app has no storage, location, contacts or microphone permissions.

Sharing

A document leaves the app only when you send it somewhere yourself, for example with Android's Share feature or by saving it to a folder you pick.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
